Support & Help

PrismaRay is a laser-optics puzzle game. Bend a beam of light through mirrors, prisms, splitters and more to light up every target. Need a hand? Start here, or email us anytime.

📧 jmamblat@duck.com

How to play

Drag an optic from the tray onto a glowing slot, then tap it to rotate and aim the beam. Guide the laser from the emitter to every target to solve the level. Use fewer optics for more stars. Tap the lightbulb for a hint, or UNDO / CLEAR to rethink.

Frequently asked questions

The timer feels tight on later levels.

Levels 1–9 are relaxed; the clock tightens from level 10 on. Prefer no pressure? Switch to Zen mode in Settings — the timer never fails you.

I’m color-blind. Can I still play the color puzzles?

Yes. Settings → Accessibility has Deuteranopia, Protanopia and Tritanopia palettes plus letter cues on colored beams and targets. There’s also a Reduce Motion option and adjustable haptics.

What is the Daily Challenge?

A new puzzle generated fresh every day, the same for everyone. Build a daily streak and, with Game Center enabled, compare your score on the leaderboard.

Can I make my own levels?

Yes — open the Editor from the main menu to paint your own puzzle, test it, save it, and share a level code with friends.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app, or resetting in Settings, clears it. PrismaRay has no accounts or cloud sync, so progress is local to each device.

There’s no sound.

Check the SOUND toggle in Settings and your device’s mute switch. PrismaRay’s music and effects respect the silent switch.
