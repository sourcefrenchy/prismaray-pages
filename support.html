How to play

Drag an optic from the tray onto a glowing slot. Tap a placed optic to rotate it. The beam redraws live, so you can see what every move does. Light all the targets to solve the level. A slot outlined with a symbol only accepts that kind of optic.

Each level shows a par. Solve it in par moves or fewer, without hints, for three stars. Within three moves of par earns two. Any solve earns one. Placing a piece is one move, each tap to rotate is one more.

Frequently asked questions

How do hints work?

You hold up to three hints at a time. One comes back every ten minutes, and every three-star solve adds one. A hint places the next correct optic for you, but a level solved with a hint caps at two stars.

Why is the next chapter locked?

Chapters open with stars. You need half the stars available in the previous chapter to start the next one. Replay a few levels and aim for par to unlock it.

Is there a timer?

Not by default. Zen mode has no clock, so take all the time you want. If you like pressure, switch to Arcade in Settings and the countdown comes back.

What are ranks, skins and beam tints?

Every solve earns XP. There are ten ranks, from Apprentice to Grandmaster. Ranks and finished chapters unlock board skins (Observatory, Lab, Neon, Paper) and beam tints, which you pick in Settings under Appearance.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Three fresh puzzles a day, Easy, Medium and Hard, the same for everyone. Solve all three to count the day toward your streak. The calendar shows your last five weeks, and you can share a result card without giving away the solution.

What is the Lab Notebook?

A codex of every optic you have met. It shows how each one bends the beam in the game and what the real thing does in a lab.

What happens after level 100?

The Beyond chapter keeps going with freshly generated levels, each checked for a fair, unique solution before you see it.

I’m color-blind. Can I still play the color puzzles?

Yes. Settings has Deuteranopia, Protanopia and Tritanopia palettes plus letter cues on colored beams and targets. There is also a Reduce Motion option, adjustable haptics, and the text follows your Dynamic Type size.

Can I make my own levels?

Yes. Open the Editor from the main menu to paint your own puzzle, test it, save it, and share a level code with friends.

What about reminders?

Reminders are off unless you turn them on in Settings. They are scheduled on your device only, and you can switch them off at any time.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app, or resetting in Settings, clears it. PrismaRay has no accounts or cloud sync, so progress is local to each device.

There’s no sound.

Check the SOUND toggle in Settings and your device’s mute switch. PrismaRay’s music and effects respect the silent switch.
