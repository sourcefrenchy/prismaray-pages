PRISMARAY

Privacy Policy · Last updated June 18, 2026

PrismaRay collects no personal data. Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. PrismaRay has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, streak, settings (sound, haptics, color-blind mode, game mode), any custom levels you create, and your Daily Challenge history are stored locally on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device through PrismaRay and is removed if you delete the app.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features (such as the Daily Challenge leaderboard and achievements), your scores and achievement progress are handled by Apple’s Game Center service under Apple’s Privacy Policy. PrismaRay does not receive or store this data on any server of ours — we operate no servers.

Children’s privacy

PrismaRay is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links out to other apps or websites within gameplay.

Third parties

PrismaRay includes no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising networks. The only Apple framework that touches anything network-related is Game Center, described above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
