Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

PrismaRay collects no personal data. Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. PrismaRay has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We operate no servers.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, XP and rank, hint bank, discovered optics, settings (sound, haptics, colour-blind mode, game mode, appearance, reminders), any custom levels you create, and your Daily Challenge history are stored locally on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device through PrismaRay and is removed if you delete the app.

Reminders

PrismaRay can show optional reminders (a daily puzzle nudge and a streak warning). They are off by default. If you turn them on, iOS asks for notification permission, and the reminders are scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification service is used and nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

The Daily Challenge result card is plain text you choose to share through the iOS share sheet. It contains only your stars and streak for the day. Where it goes is up to you and the app you share it with.

Game Center

PrismaRay does not currently use Game Center. If a future update adds leaderboards or achievements, your scores would be handled by Apple’s Game Center service under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and this page would be updated first.

Children’s privacy

PrismaRay is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links out to other apps or websites within gameplay.

Third parties

PrismaRay includes no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising networks.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
